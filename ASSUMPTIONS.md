# ASSUMPTIONS LOG

Every geometric, temporal, geochemical or data-handling assumption in the app, with its certainty tier
and the source it rests on. "Paper" = Volkert, Gorring, Peck & Stanford (2023), *Minerals* 13(10), 1249.
Tiers: **Observed** (measured/mapped), **Inferred** (the paper's interpretation), **Illustrative** (our
reconstruction, no direct source), **Mindat-reported** (user-contributed, own class).

IDs: A-G geometry, A-T time, A-C chemistry/fluids, A-D data handling, A-H hydrography, A-M Mindat.
